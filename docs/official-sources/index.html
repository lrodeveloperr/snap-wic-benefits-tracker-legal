Official Government Sources & Non-Affiliation

Government non-affiliationSNAP & WIC Benefits Tracker is an independent application. It does not represent, operate on behalf of, or have an affiliation with any government or political entity. It does not connect to government benefit accounts and does not provide official government benefit balances or eligibility determinations.

Use the following official sources to verify important SNAP, PAN, and WIC information. Government program rules, eligibility standards, retailer participation, approved products, and benefit amounts can change.
