Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

SNAP-EBT & WIC Benefits Tracker is operated by Lateef Razaq-Oyetola, an individual based in Ontario, Canada. On Google Play, the app may be published under the developer name GoodUse Studios, the Android-facing developer name used by the operator.

Privacy summaryThe app is designed around local-first use. There is no login, user account, personal profile, cloud synchronization, or publisher-operated analytics or telemetry system. Core SNAP/PAN, WIC, grocery, transaction, and shopping-budget records remain on your device unless you deliberately export a file. The app never asks for an EBT/WIC PIN and does not connect to a government benefit account. The free app uses Google AdMob, which may independently process limited advertising, device, interaction, approximate-location, and diagnostic information as described below.

1. Information stored in the tracker

Depending on how you use the app, you may enter SNAP/PAN balances; WIC benefits, quantities, categories, start and expiration dates; grocery items, quantities and prices; store names; payment or benefit allocations; shopping-budget amounts and periods; transaction history; saved baskets; reminders; and app preferences.

These tracker records are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to an operator-controlled account, analytics service, profile database, or cloud database. We do not intentionally send these records to Google AdMob as advertising or analytics parameters.

2. Identity and credential information the tracker does not request

The tracker does not create or maintain a personal profile. It has no dedicated fields for your name, case number, agency, caseworker or clinic name, street or mailing address, Social Security number, email address, phone number, date of birth, full EBT/WIC card number, EBT PIN, WIC PIN, or other case-routing identity information.

The app does not require a SNAP, PAN, EBT, or WIC account login and does not connect to government benefit accounts. Do not enter PINs, passwords, Social Security numbers, full benefit-card numbers, or full payment-card numbers into free-text labels.

3. Reports, exports, and imports

Reports are created locally and are designed without built-in participant identity, personal-profile, or case-routing fields. They may contain detailed activity such as dates, stores, grocery items, quantities, prices, benefit allocations, split payments, corrections, refunds, imported-record provenance, reconciliation information, warnings, and app identification. Because those details may still be sensitive, review and protect a report before sharing it.

App-generated reports are supporting records, not official agency forms or government records. If you provide a report to an agency or clinic, you can attach it to the agency's official form or add identifying information yourself outside the app.

You can deliberately export History to a backup file and import a compatible backup on another device. Export and import occur only when you initiate them. The app reads imports locally and does not upload History to an operator-controlled server. Imported records retain their source provenance and do not automatically modify the receiving device's current SNAP, WIC, or shopping-budget balances. Protect exported files because anyone with access to an unencrypted file may be able to read it.

4. Advertising and App Store privacy disclosures

The free app uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may process information necessary to request, deliver, secure, frequency-cap, measure, and improve ads. Depending on the device, consent choices, jurisdiction, SDK configuration, and ad response, this may include:

  • IP address and coarse location inferred from it;
  • device or app identifiers available to the SDK;
  • app launches, taps, ad views, video views, and other product interactions associated with advertising;
  • advertising data, including ads shown or interacted with;
  • non-user-related crash logs; and
  • performance information such as launch time, hang rate, or energy use.

The app does not include a separate publisher-operated analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting SDK. The analytics and diagnostic purposes described here refer to processing by Google Mobile Ads and applicable platform services.

In App Store Connect, these practices are disclosed as Coarse Location, Device ID, Product Interaction, Advertising Data, Crash Data, and Performance Data. Apple may describe some of these categories as linked to a user because Apple's definition includes linkage through a device identifier, even though the app has no named user account and the publisher does not combine this advertising data with local benefit records.

Google may use these categories for third-party advertising, analytics, ad security, measurement, and app functionality as specified in the store disclosure. See the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising configuration and privacy choices

The app is configured to request limited, non-personalized ads. It does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission or intentionally access IDFA for personalized advertising. The publisher first-party ID is disabled, and the app does not initialize publisher-selected Google-controlled mediation partners. The iOS package may include SKAdNetwork identifiers to support Apple's privacy-preserving app-install attribution framework.

Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no technical advertising data is processed. Google may still use contextual information and limited device, interaction, approximate-location, advertising, diagnostic, security, and measurement data.

The app may use Google User Messaging Platform and related Google privacy controls where required by applicable law or Google policy. Where available, you can reopen those choices from Legal & Support → Privacy Choices.

6. Optional permanent Remove Ads purchases

The app may offer an optional, one-time, non-consumable Remove Ads purchase on each platform. The intended U.S. storefront prices are USD $4.99 on iPhone and USD $3.99 on Android. Apple or Google may display an equivalent local price, currency, and applicable tax based on the user's storefront. These purchases are not subscriptions.

Apple processes iPhone payments through the App Store, and Google processes Android payments through Google Play. The app may receive StoreKit or Google Play Billing product, transaction, entitlement, and purchase-status information needed to complete, verify, retain, or restore the permanent ad-removal entitlement. The operator does not receive complete payment-card details. A purchase does not expose or transmit local SNAP/PAN, WIC, grocery, budget, History, backup, or report records.

Each purchase is a separate platform entitlement associated with the applicable store account; an iPhone purchase does not automatically unlock Android, and an Android purchase does not automatically unlock iPhone. If verification remains entirely within Apple or Google platform services and on the device, the operator does not receive the transaction record through a separate operator-controlled server. If a future version sends transaction information to an external verification service, this policy and the applicable store privacy disclosure must be updated for that release.

7. Support communications

The app has no in-app support account or support profile. A support link may open your device's email application. If you voluntarily send an email, your email provider sends the address and content you choose to provide directly to us outside the app. Do not include benefit credentials, PINs, Social Security numbers, full card numbers, or other sensitive credentials.

Support correspondence received outside the app may be retained only as reasonably necessary to respond, keep business records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

8. External websites and resources

The app links to government agencies, nonprofit organizations, retailers, assistance programs, and other external resources. When you open an external site, its operator may process information under its own privacy policy. We do not control those external privacy practices.

9. Retention and deletion

Core tracker records remain locally stored until you change or delete them. Use Settings → Clear All Data to clear the active tracker state and return the app to initial setup. If you previously used an older app version, a local migration copy may remain until a compatible update removes it or you remove the app and its data using your operating system's controls. Because there is no app user account, there is no separate cloud account to delete.

History backups and reports that you save or share are outside the app's local storage. Delete copies from Files, cloud storage, email, messaging apps, or other destinations yourself when you no longer want them retained.

10. Security

We minimize first-party data handling by keeping core tracker information local and rejecting dedicated identity-profile fields from backup and report data structures. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Information processed by Apple, Google, email providers, or external sites is also subject to those providers' security practices.

11. Adults only

The app is intended only for people age 18 or older, including adult benefit participants, parents, guardians, and household shoppers. It is not directed to children or minors.

12. Canadian privacy obligations and international processing

The operator is based in Ontario, Canada. Where Canadian private-sector privacy law applies to information under our control, we handle it in accordance with applicable requirements. Apple, Google, and their service providers may process their own platform or advertising information in Canada, the United States, or other jurisdictions where they operate.

13. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app, SDKs, advertising configuration, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
