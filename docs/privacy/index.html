Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

SNAP-EBT & WIC Benefits Tracker is operated by Lateef Razaq-Oyetola, an individual based in Ontario, Canada. On Google Play, the app may be published under the developer name GoodUse Studios, the Android-facing developer name used by the operator.

Privacy promiseThe app is designed around local, anonymous use. There is no login, user account, personal profile, analytics system, telemetry system, behavioral profile, or publisher-operated user tracker. Core SNAP/PAN, WIC, grocery, transaction, and shopping-budget records remain on your device unless you deliberately export a file. The app never asks for an EBT/WIC PIN and does not connect to a government benefit account. Reports are anonymous local activity summaries. Advertising is configured for limited, non-personalized delivery.

1. Information you enter into the tracker

Depending on how you use the app, you may enter SNAP/PAN balances; WIC benefits, quantities, categories, start and expiration dates; grocery items, quantities and prices; store names; payment or benefit allocations; shopping-budget amounts and periods; transaction history; saved baskets; reminders; and app preferences.

These records are designed to be stored locally on your device rather than uploaded to an operator-controlled account, analytics service, profile database, or cloud database.

2. Identity and profile information the app does not collect

The app does not create or maintain a personal profile. It has no fields for your name, case number, agency, caseworker or clinic name, street or mailing address, Social Security number, email address, phone number, date of birth, full EBT/WIC card number, EBT PIN, WIC PIN, or other case-routing or identity profile information.

The app does not require a SNAP, PAN, EBT, or WIC account login and does not connect to government benefit accounts. Do not enter PINs, passwords, Social Security numbers, full benefit-card numbers, or full payment-card numbers into free-text labels.

3. Anonymous reports

Reports generated by the app are anonymous, local-only activity summaries. They can contain report purpose and date range, program and jurisdiction, transaction and benefit activity, split-payment details, corrections, refunds, imported-record provenance, reconciliation information, warnings, and app/publisher identification. They do not contain participant identity, personal profile, or case-routing fields.

If you choose to provide a report to an agency or clinic, you can attach it to the agency's official form or add any identifying information yourself outside the app. App-generated reports are supporting records and are not official agency forms or government records.

4. No publisher analytics, telemetry, or behavioral tracking

The app itself does not include a publisher analytics SDK, telemetry service, behavioral profiling system, cross-app tracking system, or advertising-identifier permission flow. It does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission or intentionally access IDFA for personalized advertising. It does not use SKAdNetwork configuration for app-install conversion attribution.

The app does not send SNAP/PAN balances, WIC records, grocery history, transaction history, shopping-budget values, History backups, report contents, or user-entered labels to the publisher for analytics or profiling.

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

The free version uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The native integration is privacy-locked for limited, non-personalized ads. Ad personalization is not a user or build-time option. The integration disables the publisher first-party ID, disables Google Mobile Ads SDK crash reporting, does not initialize Google-controlled mediation, does not request ATT permission, and does not add SKAdNetwork attribution identifiers.

Using an advertising SDK still requires technical ad delivery and measurement. Google may process information needed to request, deliver, secure, frequency-cap, and measure ads, which can include IP address or general location derived from it, app or device identifiers available to the SDK, ad impressions and interactions, app interactions associated with ad delivery, and performance information. This third-party technical processing is separate from the app's own local benefit-tracking records and is governed by Google's policies.

See Google Privacy Policy. We do not intentionally send core benefit, grocery, budget, backup, or report data to AdMob as targeting parameters.

6. Advertising privacy choices

The app may use Google User Messaging Platform and related Google privacy controls where required by applicable law or Google policy. Where available, you can reopen those choices from Legal & Support → Privacy Choices. These controls do not turn on personalized advertising in this app.

7. History export and import

You can deliberately export History to a backup file and import a compatible backup on another device. Export happens only when you initiate it. A backup can contain transaction dates, store names, grocery items, quantities, prices, payment or benefit allocations, and other History details. The app reads imports locally; the feature does not upload History to an operator-controlled server.

Imported records preserve their source provenance, are treated separately from current local balances, and do not automatically modify the receiving device's current SNAP, WIC, or shopping-budget balances. Protect exported files because anyone who can access an unencrypted backup may be able to read its contents.

8. Purchases

The app may offer a one-time purchase to remove ads. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, not directly by us. The app may receive product, transaction, entitlement, or purchase-status information needed to verify or restore the purchase. We do not receive your complete payment-card details.

9. Support communications

The app has no in-app support account or support profile. A support link may open your device's email application. If you voluntarily send an email, your email provider sends the address and content you choose to provide directly to us outside the app. Do not include benefit credentials, PINs, Social Security numbers, full card numbers, or other sensitive credentials in support email.

Support correspondence received outside the app may be retained only as reasonably necessary to respond, keep business records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

10. External websites and resources

The app links to government agencies, nonprofit organizations, retailers, assistance programs, and other external resources. When you open an external site, its operator may process information under its own privacy policy. We do not control those external privacy practices.

11. Retention, deletion, and exported files

Core tracker records remain locally stored until you change or delete them. Use Settings → Clear All Data to remove locally stored tracker data and return the app to initial setup. Because there is no app user account, there is no separate cloud account to delete.

History backups and reports that you save or share are outside the app's local storage. Delete copies from Files, cloud storage, email, messaging apps, or other destinations yourself when you no longer want them retained.

12. Security

We minimize first-party data handling by keeping core tracker information local and by rejecting identity/profile fields from app backup and report data structures. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Information processed by Apple, Google, or external sites is also subject to those providers' security practices.

13. Children

The app is intended for adults, including adult benefit participants, parents, guardians, and household shoppers. It is not directed to children under 13.

14. Canadian privacy obligations and international processing

The operator is based in Ontario, Canada. Where Canadian private-sector privacy law applies to information under our control, we handle it in accordance with applicable requirements. Apple, Google, and their service providers may process their own platform or advertising information in Canada, the United States, or other jurisdictions where they operate.

15. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app, SDKs, advertising configuration, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
