Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

SNAP-EBT & WIC Benefits Tracker is operated by Lateef Razaq-Oyetola, an individual based in Ontario, Canada. On Google Play, the app may be published under the developer name Gooduse Studios, the Android-facing developer name used by the operator.

Privacy summaryThe core SNAP/PAN, WIC, grocery, transaction, and shopping-budget information that you enter into the tracker is designed to remain locally stored on your device. The app does not require an account, does not request an EBT/WIC PIN, and does not connect to a government benefit account. You can choose to export and import History files between devices. The free version uses Google AdMob configured to request non-personalized ads.

1. Information you enter into the tracker

Depending on how you use the app, you may enter SNAP/PAN balances; WIC benefits, quantities, categories, start dates and expiration dates; grocery items, quantities and prices; store names; payment or benefit allocations; shopping-budget amounts and periods; transaction history; saved baskets; reminders; and application preferences.

These core tracking records are designed to be stored locally on your device rather than uploaded to an operator-controlled account or cloud database.

History export and import. You can choose to export transaction History to a file so you can keep a backup or import it on another device. Export happens only when you initiate it. An exported History file may contain transaction dates, store names, grocery items, quantities, prices, payment or benefit allocations, and other History details recorded in the app. You are responsible for storing and sharing exported files securely. When you import a compatible History backup, the app reads the file on the device and adds valid History records. Imported records are read-only and are designed not to modify the current SNAP, WIC, or shopping-budget balances on the receiving device. This feature does not upload your History to an operator-controlled server.

2. Information the app does not ask for

The app does not require a SNAP, PAN, EBT, or WIC account login. It does not ask for your EBT PIN or WIC PIN and does not connect to government benefit accounts. Please do not enter PINs, passwords, Social Security numbers, or full payment-card numbers into free-text fields or support messages.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The free version uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app is configured to request non-personalized ads only. We do not use previously collected or historical advertising data to personalize ad selection.

Non-personalized advertising can still involve processing such as IP address or general location derived from it, device or app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, app interactions, diagnostics, and performance information. Google states that non-personalized ads may still use cookies or mobile identifiers for purposes such as frequency capping and aggregated ad reporting.

Google processes information under its own terms and privacy policies. See Google Privacy Policy.

We do not intentionally send SNAP/PAN balances, WIC benefit records, grocery history, transaction history, shopping-budget values, or exported History contents to AdMob as ad-targeting parameters.

4. Advertising privacy choices

The app does not intentionally request personalized advertising. The native ad integration is configured to request non-personalized ads. The app does not use App Tracking Transparency permission as a mechanism to enable personalized advertising.

The app may use Google User Messaging Platform and related Google privacy controls to obtain or communicate privacy choices required by applicable law or Google policy. Where applicable, you can reopen advertising privacy choices from the app's Legal & Support → Privacy Choices control.

5. U.S. state privacy terminology

We do not sell the core SNAP/PAN, WIC, grocery-history, transaction-history, or budget records you manually enter into the tracker. AdMob may process advertising-related device information as described above even when ads are non-personalized. Where applicable, privacy choices or opt-out mechanisms are made available through the app or the advertising platform.

6. Purchases

The app may offer a one-time purchase to remove ads. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, not directly by us. We do not receive your complete payment-card details. The app or platform may receive product, transaction, entitlement, or purchase-status information necessary to verify or restore the purchase.

7. Support communications

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, screenshots, and device or app details. Support correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond, keep business records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

8. External websites and resources

The app links to government agencies, nonprofit organizations, retailers, assistance programs, and other external resources. When you open an external site, its operator may collect information under its own privacy policy. We do not control those external privacy practices.

9. Retention, deletion, and exported files

Core tracker records are designed to remain locally stored until you change or delete them. Use Settings → Clear All Data to remove locally stored tracker data and return the app to initial setup. Because the app currently has no user account, there is no separate SNAP-EBT & WIC Benefits Tracker cloud account to delete.

History files that you export are outside the app's local storage once saved or shared. Delete copies from Files, cloud storage, email, messaging apps, or other destinations yourself when you no longer want them retained.

Advertising information processed by Google is retained according to Google's applicable policies and user settings. Support information under our control is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the service and minimize first-party data handling by keeping core tracker information local. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Information processed by Apple, Google, or external sites is also subject to those providers' security practices.

11. Children

The app is intended for adults, including adult benefit participants, parents, guardians, and household shoppers. It is not directed to children under 13. If we learn that personal information has been provided directly to us by a child in circumstances requiring deletion, contact us at lrodeveloperr@gmail.com.
