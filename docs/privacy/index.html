Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 8, 2026

SNAP & WIC Benefits Tracker is operated by Lateef Razaq-Oyetola, an individual based in Ontario, Canada. On Google Play, the app may be published under the developer name Gooduse Studios, the Android-facing developer name used by the operator.

Privacy summaryThe core SNAP/PAN, WIC, grocery, transaction, and shopping-budget information that you enter into the tracker is designed to remain locally stored on your device. The app does not require an account, does not request an EBT/WIC PIN, and does not connect to a government benefit account. The free version uses Google AdMob, which processes certain device, advertising, network, interaction, and diagnostic information.

1. Information you enter into the tracker

Depending on how you use the app, you may enter SNAP/PAN balances; WIC benefits, quantities, categories, start dates and expiration dates; grocery items, quantities and prices; store names; payment or benefit allocations; shopping-budget amounts and periods; transaction history; saved baskets; reminders; and application preferences.

In the version covered by this policy, these core tracking records are designed to be stored locally on your device rather than uploaded to an operator-controlled account or cloud database.

2. Information the app does not ask for

The app does not require a SNAP, PAN, EBT, or WIC account login. It does not ask for your EBT PIN or WIC PIN and does not connect to government benefit accounts. Please do not enter PINs, passwords, Social Security numbers, or full payment-card numbers into free-text fields or support messages.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The free version uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Depending on the platform, device settings, permissions, privacy choices, and Google configuration, Google advertising technologies may automatically process information such as IP address or general location derived from it, device or advertising identifiers, advertisement impressions and interactions, app interactions, diagnostics, and performance information.

Google processes information under its own terms and privacy policies. See Google Privacy Policy.

We do not intentionally send the SNAP/PAN balances, WIC benefit records, grocery history, transaction history, or shopping-budget values you enter into the core tracker to AdMob as ad-targeting parameters.

4. Personalized advertising and tracking choices

The app may use personalized advertising where permitted and where required permissions or privacy choices allow it. Personalized ads can be selected using previously collected or historical advertising-related information.

Apple devices

Where Apple's App Tracking Transparency requirements apply, the app requests permission before accessing the device's advertising identifier for tracking. If you deny permission, you may continue using the app and ads may still be requested without the IDFA being sent in the ad request.

Android devices and U.S. state privacy choices

The app may use Google User Messaging Platform and related Google privacy controls to obtain or communicate applicable privacy choices. Where required, Google technologies may use restricted data processing or Global Privacy Platform signals.

Where applicable, you can reopen advertising privacy choices from the app's Legal & Support / Privacy Choices control.

5. U.S. state privacy terminology

We do not sell the core SNAP/PAN, WIC, grocery-history, or budget records you manually enter into the tracker. Some U.S. privacy laws may define certain transfers of device or advertising identifiers for personalized advertising as a “sale,” “sharing,” or targeted advertising even when no money changes hands. Where applicable, privacy choices or opt-out mechanisms are made available through the app or the advertising platform.

6. Purchases

The app may offer a one-time purchase to remove ads. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, not directly by us. We do not receive your complete payment-card details. The app or platform may receive product, transaction, entitlement, or purchase-status information necessary to verify or restore the purchase.

7. Support communications

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, screenshots, and device or app details. Support correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond, keep business records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

8. External websites and resources

The app links to government agencies, nonprofit organizations, retailers, assistance programs, and other external resources. When you open an external site, its operator may collect information under its own privacy policy. We do not control those external privacy practices.

9. Retention and deletion

Core tracker records are designed to remain locally stored until you change or delete them. Use Settings → Clear All Data to remove locally stored tracker data and return the app to initial setup. Because the app currently has no user account, there is no separate SNAP & WIC Benefits Tracker cloud account to delete.

Advertising information processed by Google is retained according to Google's applicable policies and user settings. Support information under our control is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the service and minimize first-party data handling by keeping core tracker information local. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Information processed by Apple, Google, or external sites is also subject to those providers' security practices.

11. Children

The app is intended for adults, including adult benefit participants, parents, guardians, and household shoppers. It is not directed to children under 13. If we learn that personal information has been provided directly to us by a child in circumstances requiring deletion, contact us at lrodeveloperr@gmail.com.

12. Canadian privacy obligations

The operator is based in Ontario, Canada. Where Canadian private-sector privacy law applies to information under our control, we handle it in accordance with applicable requirements concerning accountability, appropriate purposes, meaningful consent, limiting collection, limiting use and retention, safeguards, openness, individual access, and complaint handling.

13. International processing

Apple, Google, advertising partners, and their service providers may process information in Canada, the United States, or other jurisdictions where they operate. Information processed outside your jurisdiction may be subject to the laws and lawful-access requirements of that jurisdiction.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, SDKs, advertising configuration, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
