Purchases

iOS. Remove Ads Forever is a one-time, non-consumable App Store purchase. The U.S. list price is US$9.99; the App Store shows the price and currency for your storefront before confirmation. It is not a subscription. Open Remove Ads in the sidebar to purchase it.

To recover a previous purchase made with the same Apple Account, use Settings → Restore Purchase. Restoring does not charge you again. The banner remains hidden only while Apple reports a valid entitlement. If Apple refunds or revokes the purchase, or otherwise reports that the entitlement is no longer valid, ads may return. Apple handles billing and refunds; do not email payment-card details. Android. The current Android version does not offer subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Move History to another device

Export a History backup, then import the compatible file on the receiving device. Duplicates are skipped. Imported History is designed not to change current SNAP, WIC, or shopping-budget balances. The operator cannot recover a forgotten encrypted-backup passphrase. Protect exported files and remember that a share destination has its own terms and privacy practices.

Delete local tracker data

Settings → Clear All Data permanently removes this app's locally stored tracker data from this device, removes recognized legacy/recovery tracker state and surviving temporary export-cache copies, cancels its scheduled local reminders, and returns you to initial setup. On Android, it also resets the locally stored publisher advertising choice. Files you previously saved or shared to separate locations are not deleted.

On iOS, Clear All Data does not cancel, refund, or delete an App Store purchase or entitlement. It does not revoke iOS notification permission or necessarily clear Google's separate advertising-consent record; use iOS Settings and Privacy Choices where available.

Notifications and Privacy Mode

Notification content can appear on the lock screen and reveal use of SNAP/PAN or WIC. On iOS, adjust notification previews in iOS Settings; on Android, use Android's system notification settings. Privacy Mode masks selected on-screen values only; it does not add separate encryption beyond operating-system protections.

Advertising privacy choices

iOS. Without a verified Remove Ads entitlement, the app may request one fixed non-personalized banner when Google reports that an ad request is permitted. Privacy Choices opens Google's UMP controls and changes the advertising-data choices available for this device. It is not an ad on/off switch and does not purchase or restore Remove Ads.

Android. The publisher advertising choice is optional and off by default. Declining does not block any core tracker feature. The app does not contact Google for consent information or request an ad while that choice is off. If you turn it on, non-personalized ads can still involve device, coarse-location, advertising, interaction, performance, diagnostic, and consent data processed by Google. Turn it off in Legal & Support to block future Android ad requests; where available, Privacy Choices reopens Google's separate controls.
