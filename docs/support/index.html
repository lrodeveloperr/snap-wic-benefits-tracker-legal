Remove Ads purchases and restoration

Remove Ads is an optional, permanent, non-consumable purchase. The intended U.S. storefront price is USD $4.99 on iPhone and USD $3.99 on Android. Neither purchase is a subscription. Apple or Google displays and processes the final storefront price.

Purchases are platform-specific and tied to the applicable store account. If ads return after reinstalling the app or moving to another supported device on the same platform, confirm that you are signed into the Apple or Google account used for the purchase and select Restore Purchase or the corresponding restoration function. If restoration still fails, contact support with the platform, app version, and error description. Do not email payment-card details, store-account passwords, or complete transaction documents.

Move History to another device

Use the History screen's export option to create a backup file, then use Import History on the receiving device. Compatible imported records are added to History while duplicates are skipped. Imported History is read-only and is designed not to change the receiving device's current SNAP, WIC, or shopping-budget balances. Keep exported files private because they may contain store, item, price, and transaction details.

Delete local tracker data

The app does not require a user account. Use Settings → Clear All Data to clear the active tracker state and return to initial setup. If you previously used an older app version, a local migration copy may remain until a compatible update removes it or you remove the app and its data through your operating system. Files exported outside the app are stored separately and must be deleted from their saved locations.

Advertising privacy choices

The app is configured to request limited, non-personalized ads. Non-personalized advertising can still involve limited device, approximate-location, advertising, interaction, performance, and diagnostic data processed by Google. Where available or required, use Legal & Support → Privacy Choices to review advertising privacy options.
