Remove Ads purchase help

For purchase or restoration problems, first confirm that you are signed into the same Apple ID or Google Play account used for the purchase. If the app still does not recognize the entitlement, contact support with the platform and a description of the issue. Do not email payment-card information.

Move History to another device

Use the History screen's export option to create a backup file, then use Import History on the receiving device. Compatible imported records are added to History while duplicates are skipped. Imported History is read-only and is designed not to change the receiving device's current SNAP, WIC, or shopping-budget balances. Keep exported files private because they may contain store, item, price, and transaction details.

Delete local tracker data

The app currently does not require a user account. To remove locally stored tracker information, use Settings → Clear All Data. This returns the tracker to initial setup. Files you previously exported outside the app are stored separately and must be deleted from their saved location if you no longer want them.

Advertising privacy choices

The app is configured to request non-personalized ads. Where available or required, use the app's Legal & Support → Privacy Choices control to review advertising privacy options. Google or operating-system privacy controls may also be available depending on your device and jurisdiction.

Government program questions

We cannot make official eligibility, balance, retailer, or product-authorization decisions. For authoritative information, use the Official Government Sources.
