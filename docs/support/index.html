Purchases

The current version does not offer subscriptions or in-app purchases. If a future version adds a purchase, the app, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, support information, and store disclosures will be updated before release.

Move History to another device

Export a History backup, then import the compatible file on the receiving device. Duplicates are skipped. Imported History is designed not to change current SNAP, WIC, or shopping-budget balances. The operator cannot recover a forgotten encrypted-backup passphrase. Protect exported files and remember that a share destination has its own terms and privacy practices.

Delete local tracker data

Settings → Clear All Data permanently removes this app's locally stored tracker data from this device, removes recognized legacy/recovery tracker state and surviving temporary export-cache copies, cancels its scheduled local reminders, resets the locally stored publisher advertising choice, and returns you to initial setup. Files you previously saved or shared to separate locations are not deleted. It does not revoke iOS notification permission or necessarily clear Google's separate advertising-consent record; use iOS Settings and Privacy Choices where available.

Notifications and Privacy Mode

Notification content can appear on the lock screen and reveal use of SNAP/PAN or WIC. Adjust notification previews in iOS Settings. Privacy Mode masks selected on-screen values only; it does not add separate encryption beyond operating-system protections.

Advertising privacy choices

The publisher advertising choice is optional and off by default. Declining does not block any core tracker feature. The app does not contact Google for consent information or request an ad while that choice is off. If you turn it on, non-personalized ads can still involve device, coarse-location, advertising, interaction, performance, diagnostic, and consent data processed by Google. Turn it off in Legal & Support to block future ad requests; where available, Privacy Choices reopens Google's separate controls.

Privacy requests

Email lrodeveloperr@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy Request” to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, withdraw consent where applicable, or make a complaint about personal information under the operator's control. We respond no later than 30 days unless a lawful extension or exception applies. If we extend the time, we will notify you of the extension and your complaint rights. See the Privacy Policy.

Government-program questions

We cannot issue official decisions about eligibility, balances, retailer acceptance, or product authorization. Use the Official Government Sources.
